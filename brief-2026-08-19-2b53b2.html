4Live Macro Themes
US-Iran & Other Major Geopolitical Conflicts (Oil)
The standing tail firmed again. Brent extended a fourth straight session of gains toward $92 as US-Iran talks to reopen the Strait of Hormuz stayed frozen, and WTI held near $84. Trump said there are no negotiations underway and the naval blockade remains in effect, three China-linked supertankers turned back, and a vessel was struck near the strait. Read it as a risk premium that is building rather than stable, with the tail that any flare-up relifts headline inflation just as the Fed is leaning hawkish and the long end is only partly relieved.
Fed, Interest Rates & Inflation
Today's minutes confirmed a hawkish tilt. The July hold was a 9-3 vote, the most fractured in years, with Hammack, Kashkari, and Logan dissenting for a quarter-point hike, and participants warning tightening "would likely be necessary if inflation did not decline." A near-term cut stays off the table, and some officials judged financial conditions still not tight enough. The one relief valve today was technical: the Treasury's larger buyback of long-dated debt pulled the 30Y to 5.19% from Tuesday's 5.32% peak. Jackson Hole Aug 27 to 29 is the next signal, with Warsh's first keynote as chair.
Trade Wars
Quiet and unchanged today, no fresh headlines. H200 and MI325X sales to China stay case-by-case under BIS, the 25% tariff on advanced chips routed through the US remains in place, and actual shipments to China stay minimal. For the book the read-through is neutral: no new escalation, though tariffs remain part of the inflation story that is keeping yields and the Fed hawkish. The structural China-supply threat to memory and foundry names persists in the background.
AI
The rotation out of AI hardware ran a second day. The SOX fell about 2.1% while the broad market rose, a clean sign this is repositioning rather than fear, with the VIX easing to 14.9. Rising yields and the unresolved capex-financing question keep pressuring the names with the least visible capex-to-free-cash-flow conversion, while the cloud story reasserted itself as AMZN gained on renewed focus on AWS at 37% growth. Nvidia's Aug 26 print is now the swing factor for whether the capex curve still compounds.
